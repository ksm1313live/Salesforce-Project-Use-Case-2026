## About Kam's Tech Talk

Kam's Tech Talk Community (#KTTC) began in 2021 as a personal initiative to support people struggling to choose or transition careers. In 2024 it evolved from an individual endeavour into a community-driven platform, dedicated to empowering individuals at various stages of their professional journey — particularly those pursuing a career in Salesforce.

The community supports young talent starting out, professionals transitioning into Salesforce roles, and those returning to the workforce after a career break.

This programme is part of that mission.

---

## Purpose

Most people learning Salesforce reach a point where they have completed training modules but have nothing practical to show. Employers ask what you have built, not what you have watched.

This programme addresses that gap. Participants design, build, document and present a working Salesforce solution, and finish with the material and confidence to talk about it in interviews.

---

## Who This Is For

This programme suits people who:

- Have completed foundational Salesforce learning (Trailhead, certification study, or equivalent hands-on experience)
- Can navigate a Salesforce org independently
- Have built at least one basic automation or data model themselves
- Are actively working towards an Admin, Business Analyst or Consultant role
- Can commit dedicated time each week to build between sessions

### Who This Is Not For (Yet)

This programme is **not** an introduction to Salesforce. It assumes working knowledge.

If you are still learning core concepts, a foundations-first route is the better starting point. This is not a rejection — it is a sequencing decision, and it produces a far better outcome. Kam's Tech Talk offers structured training programmes for Associate, Administrator and Business Analyst pathways, and we will point you to the right next step at the intake call.

## How It Works

The programme is **guided, not delivered for you**.

- Mentors provide direction, review, design input and unblocking
- Participants do the building themselves
- The finished solution is genuinely the participant's own work

This distinction matters. An artefact you built yourself holds up under interview questioning. One built for you does not.

## Programme Structure

### Phase 1 — Discovery and Scoping

**Focus:** Establishing what will be built and why.

- Select a realistic business scenario
- Capture and document requirements formally
- Define what a successful outcome looks like
- Confirm target role and the skills the project needs to evidence

**Output:** Requirements document and agreed target role

---

### Phase 2 — Solution Design

**Focus:** Designing before building.

- Data model design: objects, fields, relationships
- Entity relationship diagram
- Security model: profiles, permission sets, sharing considerations
- Process design: what is automated and what is not
- Introduction to articulating design rationale

**Output:** Solution design document and ERD

---

### Phase 3 — Build

**Focus:** Independent build with structured review.

- Participants build in their own Developer Edition org between sessions
- Configuration: objects, fields, relationships, layouts, security
- Automation: Flows, validation rules
- Reporting: reports and dashboards
- Weekly checkpoint reviews against the design document
- Technical unblocking support available

**Output:** Working Salesforce org and a build log capturing decisions made

---

### Phase 4 — Documentation and Solution Packaging

**Focus:** Turning a build into a professional deliverable.

- Full documentation pack: requirements, design, build decisions, testing notes
- Solution presentation deck: problem, approach, architecture, demo flow, outcomes
- Live demo rehearsal and feedback

**Output:** Documentation pack, solution deck and rehearsed demo

---

### Phase 5 — Career Positioning

**Focus:** Converting the project into career progress.

- CV update with the project positioned effectively
- LinkedIn profile refresh
- Certification and skills gap review against the target role
- Mock interview focused on the project: design rationale, trade-offs, lessons learned
- Job search approach and application strategy

**Output:** Updated CV and LinkedIn profile, and an interview-ready project narrative

## Support Boundaries

To keep the programme effective and fair to everyone taking part:

- Scheduled sessions follow the phase structure above
- A defined allowance of additional unblocking support is included
- Support beyond that allowance is arranged separately
- Participants are expected to complete build work between sessions
- If checkpoints are consistently missed, we pause and reassess together — this may mean extending the timeline or moving to a foundations-first route

These boundaries are set out clearly at the intake call. They are not restrictions for their own sake; they protect the quality of support each participant receives.

## Intake Process

1. **Enquiry** — Register interest
2. **Intake call** — A conversation covering your background, what you have built, your target role, prerequisites and expectations
3. **Route confirmation** — Either joining this programme, or an agreed foundations-first path
4. **Kickoff** — Scheduling, scenario selection and commencement

Every participant goes through an intake call. Places are limited to maintain the quality of individual support.
